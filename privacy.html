CASHFLOW Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026. CASHFLOW is a personal money manager provided by AGAC Private Limited, India.

What we collect

Your name, email address and mobile number to create your account, and your plan and payment status. The entries you add (accounts, transactions, categories) are encrypted on your device before they are saved.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

If you choose Continue with Google, we receive your name and email to sign you in. With your permission (the drive.file scope), the app creates one spreadsheet named "CASHFLOW Data" in your own Google Drive and keeps your entries saved there. The app can only see files it created itself; it cannot read any other file in your Drive. You can remove this access anytime from your Google Account settings.

How your data is stored

Your entries are kept encrypted on your device so you can add entries without internet, an encrypted copy is saved on our server so you can open your data on any device, and, if you signed in with Google, a copy is saved in your own Google Sheet.

What we never do

We do not sell or share your data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

You can delete all your data from inside the app. To delete your account, email us and we will remove it.
